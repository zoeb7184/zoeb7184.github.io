Career OS

A production MLOps system that ingests real job postings daily and powers an ATS resume analyser and a RAG-based career advisor.

Overview

Career OS is a production MLOps system built around 13 autonomous nodes that together ingest, clean, analyse, and surface job market data — turning a noisy stream of real job postings into resume feedback and forward-looking skill demand forecasts.

Problem

Job seekers typically get resume advice disconnected from what the market is actually asking for right now, and market-demand reports are usually static and months out of date by the time they're published.

Approach

A Prefect-orchestrated ETL pipeline ingests 656+ real job listings every day, deduplicating them with SHA-256 content hashing and normalizing extracted skills against a controlled vocabulary of 85 canonical skills. An MLflow-tracked ATS resume analyser scores resumes against live postings, while a retrieval-augmented career advisor — backed by a Qdrant vector index and Groq-hosted Llama 3 — answers career questions grounded in that same live data. A forecasting module projects skill demand over rolling 30/60/90-day windows. The Next.js frontend and FastAPI backend are deployed together on Railway, with Redis handling caching between the 13 nodes.

Results

The pipeline runs autonomously in production, ingesting 656+ real listings per day across its 13 nodes, with a publicly deployed frontend serving live ATS scoring, RAG career advice, and skill-demand forecasts.

Challenges

Job listings scraped from multiple sources arrive in wildly inconsistent formats, so reliable deduplication required SHA-256 hashing of normalized content rather than naive text matching. Keeping the 85-skill canonical vocabulary consistent as new postings introduced new phrasing needed a dedicated normalization layer between ingestion and storage, and coordinating 13 independent nodes without them stepping on each other's state was the core orchestration challenge Prefect was chosen to solve.
