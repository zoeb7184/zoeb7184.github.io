CNNs vs. Transformers Sentiment Analysis

A comparative study fine-tuning BERT against CNN architectures for sentiment analysis, reaching 90.14% accuracy on SST-2.

Python PyTorch BERT HuggingFace TensorFlow NLP SST-2
GitHub → ← Back to Projects

Overview

This project directly compares two very different approaches to sentiment classification — transformer-based BERT and convolutional neural networks — on the same benchmark, to see which generalizes better under a comparable training budget.

Problem

It's easy to assume transformers always win, but that assumption deserves testing on a concrete task with matched data and evaluation, rather than taken on faith from unrelated benchmarks.

Approach

BERT-base (via Hugging Face, trained in PyTorch) was fine-tuned against CNN architectures (built in TensorFlow) on the SST-2 sentiment dataset — 67,349 labelled sentences. BERT fine-tuning used progressive layer freezing (gradually unfreezing deeper layers rather than all at once) combined with regularization to avoid overfitting, and both model families went through a systematic hyperparameter search rather than a single fixed configuration.

Results

BERT reached 90.14% accuracy on SST-2, outperforming the CNN architectures tested under the same evaluation protocol.

Challenges

Progressive layer freezing is a balancing act: unfreeze too fast and you risk catastrophic forgetting of BERT's pretrained representations; unfreeze too slowly and the model under-adapts to the sentiment task. Tuning that unfreezing schedule, alongside the hyperparameter search across two structurally different model families, was the core engineering challenge.
