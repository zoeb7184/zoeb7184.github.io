Zoeb's Journey

I grew up in Mumbai, a city with more people, traffic and data than anyone can keep track of. Trying to make sense of it led me to a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Rizvi College of Engineering, University of Mumbai, and then to an AI/ML internship at YBI Foundation, where I built ETL pipelines on GCP BigQuery and automated SQL workflows.

In November 2024 I moved to Bielefeld for my M.Sc. in Data Science at Universität Bielefeld. I swapped Mumbai’s monsoon for German drizzle and have no regrets about the data science part.

Today I work as a research assistant (HiWi) at the university, and in my own time I build things like a RAG career advisor, an electricity price forecaster and a football analytics platform. Next on the list is a Werkstudent or internship role in Germany.

From Mumbai to Bielefeld, one dataset at a time.
